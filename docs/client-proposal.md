## 5. External and recurring costs (not included)

The fixed price covers Nexium software implementation only. Excluded:

- Hosting, domain, SSL, business email
- SMS, WhatsApp, email, telephony, AI/API, OCR, and storage usage
- Payment-gateway, banking, and transaction charges
- Biometric/GPS hardware, SIM/data, maps APIs, printers, scanners, networking
- Google Play Console fees, third-party subscriptions, taxes
- Copyrighted books/board content licensing
- Bulk data entry or cleansing beyond agreed templates

Nexium provides software-side integration against supported APIs. Hardware, vendor certification, banking onboarding, and third-party approvals remain the client's responsibility.

## 7. Client responsibilities

- Nominate one project representative and provide branding, structure, fee/payroll/accounting rules, and clean data templates
- Arrange third-party accounts, hardware, vendor access, banking approvals, and a verified Google Play Console account
- Review work promptly, support UAT/training, approve milestones in writing, and maintain hosting/subscriptions after go-live
- Ensure legal authority to use all supplied content and personal data

## 8. Assumptions and exclusions

- One institution and its branches in one dedicated deployment
- Android apps for parents and students included; iOS and offline mode excluded
- Play Store approval timing is controlled by Google
- Data import limited to clean agreed templates; historical cleanup/manual entry excluded
- Integrations require a stable documented API; unsupported proprietary hardware/firmware excluded
- No uptime percentage guaranteed; out-of-scope changes need written approval
- Nexium retains platform/source IP; client owns its data, branding, and supplied content; source-code transfer not included unless agreed separately

## 9. Acceptance, support, and change control

**Accepted when:** agreed workflows work for authorized roles without critical defects; client submits one issue list; critical issues are fixed or agreed; written approval is given, or no critical scope issue is raised within five business days.

Minor cosmetic issues do not block acceptance. Included support: remote training, UAT help, and 30-day defect fixes for accepted scope. Not included: daily operation, new modules after acceptance, third-party outages, or 24/7 onsite support.

Out-of-scope requests require a written change request covering price and timeline impact before work starts.
