# Educational Institution Management System — Feature Details

**Prepared by:** Nexium Technologies  
**Related document:** [client-proposal.md](client-proposal.md)

This document describes the functional capabilities of the proposed Educational Institution Management System. It is a technical and product appendix and does not contain commercial pricing, client identity, or contract terms.

---

## 1. Front office and admissions

- Admission inquiries and lead tracking.
- Applicant follow-up and enrollment workflow.
- Student admission with configurable institution-specific fields.
- Visitor registration and visitor history.
- Complaints, requests, and resolution tracking.
- Reception and front-office activity records.
- Announcements and instant communication workflows.
- Admission and inquiry reporting.

## 2. Student information system

- Complete student profiles with photographs and contact information.
- Guardian and sibling relationships.
- CNIC/B-Form and institution-specific information.
- Branch, program, class/batch, section, subject, and session assignments.
- Academic history and performance records.
- Digital student documents and categorized file storage.
- Student certificates and printable ID cards.
- Student status, transfer, withdrawal, and archival records.
- Search, filtering, pagination, and exports.

## 3. HR and staff management

- Employee and teacher profiles.
- Digital employee files and supporting documents.
- Role, branch, department, and designation assignment.
- Employment and contract information.
- Staff attendance and attendance reporting.
- Employee leave requests and approval workflow.
- Staff performance evaluation records.
- Payroll-related employee information.
- Role-based account invitations and account deactivation.

## 4. Attendance management

- Student, teacher, and staff attendance.
- Daily and bulk attendance marking.
- Mobile-ready attendance workflow.
- Batch/class and date-based attendance sheets.
- Attendance history, summaries, and reports.
- Parent and student attendance visibility.
- AI-assisted attendance register image scanning with human confirmation.
- Biometric-device integration readiness through supported vendor APIs.
- Attendance alerts and absence communication workflows.

## 5. Academic management

- Programs, classes/batches, sections, subjects, and academic sessions.
- Curriculum and syllabus records.
- Subject assignment by class/batch.
- Teacher and classroom assignment.
- Weekly timetable by class, teacher, and room.
- Timetable conflict detection.
- Configurable grading structures.
- Academic terminology configurable for a school, college, or coaching center.
- Multi-branch academic configuration.

## 6. Examinations and results

- Assessment, test, quiz, examination, and series planning.
- Configurable assessment types and total marks.
- Marks entry by class/batch and subject.
- Grades, percentages, rankings, and result summaries.
- Weak-subject and at-risk student indicators.
- Result publishing to authorized users.
- Digital and printable report cards.
- Answer keys and result exports.
- Teacher review and correction before publication.

## 7. Fees and finance operations

- Configurable fee types and fee structures.
- Student invoices and payment records.
- Discounts, concessions, scholarships, and late fines.
- Due dates, outstanding balances, and defaulter reports.
- Payment receipts and collection summaries.
- Parent and student fee visibility.
- SMS/WhatsApp fee reminder workflows.
- Online payment-gateway integration through a supported provider.
- Daily, monthly, branch-level, and consolidated collection reports.

## 8. Library management

- Book catalog and category management.
- Author, publisher, edition, and accession details.
- Copy and inventory tracking.
- Student and staff borrowing.
- Issue, return, renewal, and reservation records.
- Overdue tracking and library fines.
- Member and inventory reports.

## 9. Transport management

- Vehicle, driver, conductor, and supporting-document records.
- Route, stop, and schedule management.
- Student assignment to routes and stops.
- Pickup and drop-off details.
- Trip and transport attendance records.
- Vehicle and route reports.
- GPS/live-tracking integration readiness through supported hardware and vendor APIs.

## 10. Payroll management

- Employee salary structures.
- Basic salary, allowances, bonuses, deductions, and adjustments.
- Attendance and leave inputs for payroll.
- Monthly payroll processing.
- Salary slips and payroll history.
- Payroll summaries and exports.
- Approval controls before payroll finalization.

## 11. Accounting and financial reporting

- Configurable chart of accounts.
- Cash, bank, income, expense, asset, liability, and equity accounts.
- Journal, receipt, payment, and adjustment vouchers.
- General ledger and account statements.
- Trial balance.
- Income and expense statement.
- Budget entry and budget-versus-actual reporting.
- Branch-level and consolidated financial reporting.
- Linkage of approved fee and payroll transactions to accounting entries.

## 12. Learning and online academic resources

- Notes, syllabus, learning materials, links, and downloadable resources.
- Homework and assignment publishing with due dates.
- Student and parent visibility into assigned learning content.
- Online-class scheduling and meeting-link publishing.
- Resource organization by branch, class/batch, and subject.
- Publication controls for draft and visible content.

## 13. Parent portal and Android app

Parents receive secure access through the web portal and branded Android application to:

- One or multiple linked children.
- Attendance records.
- Assessments, grades, and performance summaries.
- Fees, invoices, dues, and payment status.
- Timetable and calendar.
- Notices and announcements.
- Documents and learning resources.
- Homework and syllabus information.
- Leave-request submission and status.
- Child profile and institution information.
- AI-generated performance insights where enabled.

## 14. Student portal and Android app

Students receive secure access through the web portal and branded Android application to:

- Attendance and attendance history.
- Assessments, grades, and academic performance.
- Fee status.
- Timetable and calendar.
- Notices and announcements.
- Documents, notes, syllabus, homework, and learning resources.
- Profile information.
- AI-generated academic insights where enabled.

## 15. Communications

- Individual SMS and WhatsApp workflows.
- Bulk communication to selected audiences.
- Notice broadcasting to parents, students, and staff.
- Fee-defaulter reminders.
- Message logs and delivery status where supported by the provider.
- Institution-wide announcements through portals.

## 16. Parent and student Android applications

- Separate, branded Android applications for parents and students.
- Secure sign-in using the same authorized institution accounts.
- Parent child-switching for linked children.
- Access to the corresponding portal features listed above.
- Push-notification readiness for notices, reminders, and academic updates.
- Institution name, logo, colors, and contact information.
- Android release builds in APK and Android App Bundle formats.
- Google Play Store submission assistance using the institution's developer account.
- Responsive support for commonly used Android phone and tablet screen sizes.
- Secure API communication with the institution's dedicated deployment.

## 17. Analytics, reports, and AI assistance

- Management dashboard and operational KPIs.
- Attendance trends.
- Assessment and batch-performance analytics.
- Fee collection and defaulter reporting.
- Branch-performance comparisons.
- Student rankings and weak-subject alerts.
- Attendance-versus-performance analysis.
- Exportable reports and CSV files.
- AI-assisted at-risk student detection.
- AI-assisted student performance summaries.
- AI attendance register OCR with mandatory staff review.

## 18. Security, control, and administration

- Role-based access and permissions.
- Branch-level data isolation.
- Administrative multi-factor authentication.
- Audit trail for important user actions.
- User invitations and account lifecycle controls.
- Session visibility and revocation.
- Institution feature settings.
- Custom admission fields.
- Dedicated institutional deployment and database.

## 19. White-label configuration

- Institution name and logo.
- Primary brand color.
- Contact information.
- Institution terminology, such as Class, Batch, Program, or Session.
- Currency and timezone.
- Academic branches and structure.
- Institution-specific user roles and permissions.
- Institution domain or subdomain, where provided.

## 21. Future AI Academic Suite

Nexium Technologies intends to develop an AI-assisted academic content and marking suite with:

- Paper, quiz, and test creation based on the selected educational board.
- Alignment with class/grade, subject, syllabus, and approved textbooks.
- Configurable question types and difficulty levels.
- Marks distribution and learning-outcome alignment.
- Board-style paper patterns.
- Answer keys and multiple paper variants.
- AI-assisted marking for supported objective and subjective answers.
- Mandatory teacher review and final approval before results are published.

When these capabilities are completed and released, participating institutions receive the software feature upgrade without an additional Nexium development or feature-license fee.

This is a future roadmap benefit and:

- Has no guaranteed release date.
- Is not part of the initial go-live or acceptance criteria.
- Depends on technical feasibility, model capability, and supported content formats.
- Requires the institution to provide or authorize legally usable syllabi, paper patterns, and textbook content.
- Does not include copyrighted-content acquisition or licensing.
- Does not waive third-party AI/API, OCR, storage, infrastructure, or processing charges.
- Requires teacher oversight; AI-generated papers and marking will not be treated as final without human approval.
